# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** use [Discussions](https://github.com/Viox-Development/alice-agent/discussions).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open an [issue](https://github.com/Viox-Development/alice-agent/issues/new/choose) using one of the forms.
- **Security problems:** report them privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before asking, `alice doctor` checks your providers, keys, and missing packages, and often points at the problem.
