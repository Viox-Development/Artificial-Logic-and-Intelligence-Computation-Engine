# After takeoff
<!-- F-16C, simplified. Copy it under ~/.alice/checklists/ and change it to your own list. -->
- Landing gear: up, three green out
- Landing/taxi light: off
- Afterburner: off, power at climb setting
- Trim: check
- Fuel: quantity and flow normal, external tanks feeding
- Warning lights: none
- Altimeter: 29.92 / standard after the transition altitude
- Navigation: check steerpoint and route
- Master Arm: off until needed
